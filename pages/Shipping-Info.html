Shipping FAQs

When will my order be dispatched?

Orders placed before 3:00 PM are dispatched the same business day. Orders after 3 PM are dispatched the next morning. We ship Monday to Saturday (excluding public holidays).

Can I change my delivery address after ordering?

Yes, but only within 2 hours of placing your order. Contact us immediately via WhatsApp at +92 300 0000000. Once dispatched, the address cannot be changed.

What if my package arrives damaged?

All our shipments are insured. If your package arrives damaged, take photos immediately and contact us within 24 hours. We'll arrange a free replacement or full refund — no questions asked.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes! We currently ship to UAE, Saudi Arabia, UK, Canada, and the USA. International shipping rates are calculated at checkout based on weight and destination. Delivery takes 7–14 business days.
