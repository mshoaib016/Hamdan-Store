Kaise payment karu? JazzCash ya EasyPaisa?

Hum JazzCash, EasyPaisa, bank transfer (HBL/Meezan/MCB), aur Cash on Delivery — sab accept karte hain. Checkout par apna preferred method choose karein aur payment ka screenshot upload karein. Verification ke baad order dispatch ho jata hai.

Delivery mein kitna waqt lagta hai?

Standard delivery 2–5 business days mein hoti hai. Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad ke liye express 1–2 days mein deliver ho sakta hai. International orders 7–14 business days mein aate hain.

Kya products 100% original hain?

Bilkul. Hamdan Store par har product directly official brand distributors se sourced hai. Koi replica ya grey-market item nahi. Sab products manufacturer warranty ke saath aate hain.

Return aur refund ka kya process hai?

Hum 30-day return policy offer karte hain. Delivery ke 30 din ke andar contact karein. Product original packaging mein aur unused condition mein hona chahiye. Refund 3–5 business days mein process hoti hai.

COD (Cash on Delivery) available hai?

Haan! COD poore Pakistan mein available hai. Checkout mein "Cash on Delivery" select karein. Order confirm hone ke baad hamari team aapko call karegi verification ke liye, phir dispatch hoga.

Order track kaise karu?

Order dispatch hone ke baad tracking number WhatsApp ya email par bheja jata hai. Aap hamari website ke Track My Order page par bhi check kar sakte hain.

Order cancel ya change kar sakta hun?

Order place karne ke 2 ghante ke andar cancel ya modify kar sakte hain. WhatsApp par +92 300 0000000 par apna order ID bhejein. Dispatch ke baad cancel nahi hoga, lekin return kar sakte hain.
